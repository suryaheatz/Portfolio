How this maps to the spec, and what is assumed

Taken from your files: three segments (left, centre, right) with touch position choosing the segment; the haptic vocabulary of ripple, constant with rising force and barrier; barrier in the transition areas and on push; damper off at idle; every screen, label and placeholder from the 20 stills.

Assumed for the demo: hovering stands in for capacitive touch; hold-and-drag, mouse wheel and arrow keys stand in for rotation; click stands in for push. The three tabs work as a carousel in the order Music, Fav, Comfort: the centre tab is the current one, a left push goes to the tab on its left and a right push to the tab on its right (the order loops). Comfort reuses the Seat Modes and Temperature screens, and the carousel itself is a proposal. Centre push confirms. Which menu gets which haptic profile is a proposal, not from the spec. Value ranges (14 to 28 °C, volume 0 to 30, 12 songs, 9 list items) and the Titles, Playlists and Recently Played lists are extended from the Albums pattern. Only Media is designed under Music, so Sound and Source are locked with a barrier.
